# Batch v02 BSL — Video-Source Resolutions

Method: BSL SignBank (UCL, bslsignbank.ucl.ac.uk) entry videos downloaded and frame-inspected (ffmpeg, 3–8 fps). Entries written only where handshape, location, movement, and palm orientation were all unambiguous. All descriptions rewritten in original words.

open: VERIFIED — sources: https://bslsignbank.ucl.ac.uk/dictionary/words/open-1.html (video /media/bsl-video/OP/OPEN.mp4). Both flat hands stacked at waist, palms down; separate outward and rise to chest while palms rotate to face each other.
call: VERIFIED — sources: https://bslsignbank.ucl.ac.uk/dictionary/words/call-1.html (video /media/bsl-video/CA/CALL.mp4). Dominant Y-handshape (thumb + pinky) raised to side of face, thumb beside mouth/cheek, palm facing signer.
say: VERIFIED — sources: https://bslsignbank.ucl.ac.uk/dictionary/words/say-1.html (video /media/bsl-video/ME/MENTION.mp4). Dominant index finger taps chin (pointing up, palm down), then flicks forward to chest height.
pay: VERIFIED — sources: https://bslsignbank.ucl.ac.uk/dictionary/words/pay-2.html (video /media/bsl-video/PA/PAY.mp4). Dominant cupped/rounded fingers tap down once onto the flat non-dominant palm (palm up) at waist level.
look: SKIPPED — citation forms LOOK-1/2 are production-identical to the existing SEE entry (V-hand push from the eye); the only distinct variant (LOOK-3/VIEW, "eyesight/vision") is a different lexical item and its palm orientation / final movement could not be established with certainty from frames.
try: SKIPPED — TRY-1 video shows both fists raised to chest and lowered, but the internal movement (shake vs. push vs. hold) is ambiguous across frames; cannot describe the movement with confidence.
