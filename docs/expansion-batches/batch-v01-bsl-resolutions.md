# Batch v01 BSL — video-source resolutions (2026-10-14)

history: VERIFIED — sources: https://bslsignbank.ucl.ac.uk/dictionary/words/history-1.html (video https://bslsignbank.ucl.ac.uk/media/bsl-video/HI/HISTORY.mp4; keywords: history, ancient). Dominant flat hand palm-down sweeps up from above the non-dominant flat hand and back over the shoulder.
college: VERIFIED — sources: https://bslsignbank.ucl.ac.uk/dictionary/words/college-1.html (video https://bslsignbank.ucl.ac.uk/media/bsl-video/CO/COLLEGE.mp4; keywords: college). Both hands in I-handshape (pinky up), pinky tips at temples, move down and together until pinky tips meet at chest.
test: VERIFIED — sources: https://bslsignbank.ucl.ac.uk/dictionary/words/test-1.html (video https://bslsignbank.ucl.ac.uk/media/bsl-video/CH/CHECK.mp4; keywords: checking, testing). L-handshape (index up, thumb out) raised from chest to beside the eye, then lowered; covers check/test.
word: VERIFIED — sources: https://bslsignbank.ucl.ac.uk/dictionary/words/word-1.html (video https://bslsignbank.ucl.ac.uk/media/bsl-video/WO/WORD.mp4; keywords: word, morpheme). Index-up 1-handshape held in front of the chin, palm inward; index fingertip bends down in one quick tap.
answer: VERIFIED — sources: https://bslsignbank.ucl.ac.uk/dictionary/words/answer-1.html (video https://bslsignbank.ucl.ac.uk/media/bsl-video/AN/ANSWER.mp4; keywords: answer, reply). Non-dominant fist thumb-up held at chest; dominant index finger comes down to touch the thumb tip.
color: VERIFIED — sources: https://bslsignbank.ucl.ac.uk/dictionary/words/colour-1.html (video https://bslsignbank.ucl.ac.uk/media/bsl-video/CO/COLOUR.mp4; keywords: colour — British spelling on SignBank, entry kept as "Color" per batch list). Open hand beside the cheek, palm forward, fingers fluttering.
buy: VERIFIED — sources: https://bslsignbank.ucl.ac.uk/dictionary/words/buy-1.html (video https://bslsignbank.ucl.ac.uk/media/bsl-video/BU/BUY.mp4; keywords include buy). Dominant fist thumb-up at chest, palm inward; single sharp wrist-twist flicks the thumb from up to across toward the stationary other fist.
sell: VERIFIED — sources: https://bslsignbank.ucl.ac.uk/dictionary/words/sell-1.html (video https://bslsignbank.ucl.ac.uk/media/bsl-video/SE/SELL.mp4; keywords: sale, sell). Both fists thumb-up, palms facing each other at chest; hands come slightly together while both wrists twist, flicking the thumbs outward away from each other. Mirrors buy.
travel: VERIFIED — sources: https://bslsignbank.ucl.ac.uk/dictionary/words/travelling-1.html (video https://bslsignbank.ucl.ac.uk/media/bsl-video/TR/TRAVEL.mp4; keywords: travel, tour) + https://signbsl.com/sign/travel (video https://media.signbsl.com/videos/bsl/signstation/travel.mp4, Univ. of Bristol & Deaf Studies Trust, glossed "the act of going from one place to another"). Both sources show the same form: both hands thumb-up fists at chest, palms facing each other; dominant fist pushes forward away from the body. NOTE: words/travel-1.html on SignBank resolves to the GO-TO sign (keywords go, went) — not used.
use: SKIPPED — no dedicated USE sign on SignBank (words/use-1.html resolves to the PREFER sign, keywords prefer/preference, unrelated to "use"); signbsl's two University of Bristol USE videos show differing forms (thumb-up fist held at chest vs fist at chin/throat) with no single clear citation form.
get: VERIFIED — sources: https://bslsignbank.ucl.ac.uk/dictionary/words/get-1.html (video https://bslsignbank.ucl.ac.uk/media/bsl-video/HA/HAVE.mp4; keywords: have, got — SignBank's keyword search for "get" resolves to this sign). Curved open hand, palm inward, closes to a fist while pulling back to touch the chest. One sign covers get/have/receive.
find: VERIFIED — sources: https://bslsignbank.ucl.ac.uk/dictionary/words/find-1.html (video https://bslsignbank.ucl.ac.uk/media/bsl-video/FI/FIND.mp4; keywords: find, findings). Index-up 1-handshape beside the eye (palm inward), then hand sweeps down in front of the body while fingers curl closed, finishing in a fist at chest height.
make: VERIFIED — sources: https://bslsignbank.ucl.ac.uk/dictionary/words/make-1.html (video https://bslsignbank.ucl.ac.uk/media/bsl-video/CR/CREATE.mp4; keywords: create, creation) + https://signbsl.com/sign/make (video https://media.signbsl.com/videos/bsl/signstation/create.mp4, Univ. of Bristol & Deaf Studies Trust, for the "make or cause to be" sense, listed as same-as create). Both sources show the same form: both hands pinched (thumb+fingertips), raised from waist to chest, pinched fingertips meet at centre, small twist of the wrists against each other.
